    Grid Bot

    An automated grid trading system for BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT. It lays a ladder of buy and sell orders around price and harvests the swings, with three signals (supply and demand zones, order book liquidity, news sentiment) adjusting the grid and pausing it when conditions turn bad.
