Process

From brief to final file.

The same five steps, whether it is a logo or a full campaign.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Understand the goal, the audience and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Sketches, references and a direction worth committing to.

  3. 03

    Build

    Shape it in Illustrator or Photoshop with grids, type and colour.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Spacing, contrast and detail, until nothing feels accidental.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Clean files, ready for print, web and social.
